Why Veles

Veles Capital exists to remove the constraints that hold good companies back — the org design, operating model, commercial focus and financial discipline that decide whether a business scales or stalls.

We work shoulder-to-shoulder with founders and leadership teams across the UK, combining operator-grade experience with the clarity of an investor's lens. When the opportunity is right, we don't just advise — we invest.

The Founder

Led by a CEO who has scaled at the highest level.

Rene Schuster is the sole director of Veles Capital. Over more than 25 years at CXO level, he has led some of the world's best-known technology and telecoms businesses — from Compaq and Hewlett-Packard to Vodafone, O2 and Telefónica.

As CEO of Telefónica Deutschland he grew the company from a €3bn to a €14bn market cap, made O2 the number-one consumer brand in Germany, and led one of Europe's largest IPOs. That operator's instinct is what every Veles client gets in the room.
