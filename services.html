What We Do

Growth, investment and turnaround — under one roof.

Three connected disciplines, one operator's mindset. We meet you where you are and bring the structure, capital or speed your situation demands.

Pillar 01

Consulting for Scalable Growth

We help ambitious businesses build the structure that scaling demands. Vision gets you started; operating model, organisation and financial discipline are what let you grow without breaking.

  • Scale-readiness diagnostic across operations, org and finances
  • Operating-model and organisational redesign
  • Go-to-market sharpening and commercial focus
  • Leadership coaching and execution support
  • KPI and management-rhythm design so growth compounds
▲
Pillar 02

Growth Investment

For a small number of projects we believe in, we go beyond advice. We commit capital and hands-on time to businesses where our involvement can be the difference — fully aligned with your success.

  • Selective investment into businesses and projects we choose
  • Hands-on, operator-led involvement — not passive capital
  • Strategic, commercial and network support alongside funding
  • Alignment of incentives through shared upside

Veles Capital is not FCA-regulated. This describes our commercial involvement in selected projects and is not an offer of regulated investment services. See the notice below.

◆
Pillar 03

Triage & Restructuring

When a company is failing or has stalled, time is the enemy. We move fast to diagnose what's really wrong, stabilise the core, and rebuild a credible, fundable path forward.

  • Rapid situational diagnosis and cash/runway assessment
  • Stabilisation of the core business and key relationships
  • Operational and organisational restructuring
  • A credible turnaround and growth plan leadership can rally behind
  • Hands-on support through the most critical phase

We provide management consultancy, not insolvency, legal or regulated financial advice; we work alongside your appointed professionals where required.
