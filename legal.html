1. Company information

This website is operated by Veles Capital Ltd ("Veles Capital", "we", "us"), a private limited company registered in England & Wales under company number 16880135. Our registered office is at Woodside House, Woodside, Chilworth, Southampton, England, SO16 7LB.

Veles Capital provides management consultancy and strategic advisory services to businesses, including consulting for scalable growth, corporate triage and restructuring, and selective growth investment.

2. Regulatory status (FCA)

Veles Capital Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA).

We do not provide regulated financial services, regulated investment advice, insolvency services, legal services, or tax advice. We do not hold, control or handle client money. Our consulting and advisory services are commercial in nature and fall outside the scope of FCA regulation. Where a client engagement requires regulated, legal, insolvency or accounting services, we work alongside appropriately authorised or qualified professionals appointed by the client.

3. Financial promotions & eligible investors

From time to time Veles Capital may invest in, and invite others to co-invest in, selected business ventures. Any information about such opportunities on this website is not a financial promotion approved by an authorised person and is not an offer or invitation to the general public.

Such information is directed only at, and intended only for, persons who qualify as one or more of the following under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005:

  • certified high-net-worth individuals;
  • self-certified sophisticated investors;
  • certified sophisticated investors; and/or
  • investment professionals or other persons to whom such communications may lawfully be made.

If you do not fall within one of these categories, you should not act on any opportunity information and should seek advice from an appropriately authorised financial adviser. Self-certification on this website confirms only your own statement as to your status; it is your responsibility to ensure you genuinely qualify. Veles Capital may seek approval of specific financial promotions by an FCA-authorised person where required by law.

4. Risk warning

Don't invest unless you're prepared to lose all the money you invest. Investing in private businesses and growth-stage ventures is high-risk. In particular:

  • You could lose all of your capital. Many businesses fail, and you may not get back what you invest.
  • Investments are illiquid. You may be unable to sell or exit your investment when you wish, if at all.
  • Returns are not guaranteed and any projections are not a reliable indicator of actual results.
  • Past performance, including the prior achievements of our founder or any business, is not a reliable indicator of future results.
  • You are unlikely to be protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme or the Financial Ombudsman Service if something goes wrong.
  • You should diversify and not invest more than you can afford to lose.

7. Privacy & data protection

We take your privacy seriously and comply with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. When you contact us or submit a form, we collect the personal data you provide (such as your name, email, company and message) and use it only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss our services or opportunities with you.

We do not sell your data. We retain it only for as long as necessary for these purposes and to meet our legal obligations. Form submissions are processed on our behalf by our form provider (Formspree) and email provider. You have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, or to ask how we handle your data, email rene@velescapital.net. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
