That track record runs through senior leadership at Compaq and Hewlett-Packard, a global director role at Vodafone, and the chief-executive seat at Telefónica Deutschland (O2 Germany).

It's the same operator's instinct — knowing what it really takes to scale, restructure and grow — that we now bring to every business we work with.
