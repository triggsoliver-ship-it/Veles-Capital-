The Founder

Rene Schuster

Founder & sole director of Veles Capital. A career operator who has led, scaled and transformed some of the world's best-known technology and telecoms businesses.

A 25-year operator

From engineer to chief executive — and now in your corner.

Rene Schuster has spent more than 25 years at CXO level across telecommunications, IT, media, healthcare and retail. He began his career as a development engineer at Robert Bosch before becoming a partner at KPMG, leading consulting across Europe and the Middle East.

He went on to hold senior leadership roles at Compaq and Hewlett-Packard, and in 2004 became Global Director at Vodafone.

As CEO of Telefónica Deutschland (O2 Germany) from 2009, he led one of the most significant growth and consolidation stories in European telecoms — and one of the continent's largest IPOs.

Philosophy

"Great companies are built on structure as much as vision. I help leaders build both."

Rene founded Veles Capital to do for other businesses what he spent his career doing inside large organisations: find the constraints holding growth back, redesign the structure beneath the ambition, and stay in the room until the change is real.

Where the opportunity is compelling, that conviction extends to investment — backing select projects with capital as well as counsel.
