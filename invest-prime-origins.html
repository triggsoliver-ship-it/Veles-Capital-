The business

What Prime Origins does

Prime Origins is building the trusted infrastructure for the global hemp trade: a marketplace where verified producers connect directly with buyers, cutting out opaque intermediaries.

Every product carries an on-chain “product passport” — immutable origin, ESG and compliance data captured from seed to shelf. For an industry where provenance, sustainability and compliance are everything, that traceability is the product.

Prime Origins sits at the intersection of two strong themes: real-world-asset tokenisation and agricultural traceability. Its on-chain economy is powered by the TerraFi token, which aligns producers, buyers and investors around verified, traceable supply.
