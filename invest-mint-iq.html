The business

What Mint IQ does

Mint IQ replaces the entire outbound sales function with a single platform. It generates leads across LinkedIn and email simultaneously, re-engages prospects who go cold, delivers personalised voice-note follow-ups, and includes a built-in sales-training tool so clients actually convert the calls they receive. A pre-qualification voice-call capability is in final development, completing the full sales cycle within one platform.

No other platform in the market covers this ground. Most do one piece of the puzzle — Mint IQ does all of it, so clients stop stitching together five tools and an agency that blames each other when nothing works.

The platform runs on enterprise-grade AI infrastructure via API, which means investment capital goes into growth and distribution rather than heavy engineering. A white-label distribution channel lets partners operate Mint IQ under their own brand — a scalable acquisition model that grows without proportional cost.
